Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what Buff Beauty Bar (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this booking site, why, and the choices you have. It covers this site only, not our main website or social media.

What we collect

  • When you book or join the waitlist: your name, mobile number, email, the services, date, time and provider you choose, the number of guests, any note you add, and a special occasion if you tell us.
  • When you buy a gift card: your name, mobile and email, and the recipient’s name, email (if we’re emailing it), your message and the “from” name.
  • If you sign in: your email address and your visit history, so you can see and cancel visits.
  • From our staff: visit records (checked in, no-show, cancelled) and notes about your preferences or past visits.
  • Card details: when online payment is added, card numbers go straight to our card processor. This site and our staff never see or store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm, remind you about, change or cancel your visits.
  • To contact you about your visit, your waitlist spot, or your gift card order.
  • To apply our cancellation policy and, once card payment is set up, any fees you agreed to.
  • To give you good service (for example, remembering your preferences).
  • To keep business, tax and legal records.

We don’t sell your information or share it for other companies’ advertising. We don’t send marketing texts or emails without your permission, and you can opt out of any we send.

Who we share it with

  • Our staff see what they need for their job. Each staff login only has the access its role needs.
  • Service providers that run the site for us: our database and sign-in provider (Supabase), our web host (GitHub Pages) and our font provider (Google Fonts, which receives your device’s internet address when the page loads). When we add card payments, texts or emails, those companies (a card processor, a texting service and an email service) get only what they need to do that job.
  • Gift card recipients see the names and message on the gift card. Anyone with a gift card link or code can see that card, so share it only with the person it’s for.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety or our rights.

Stored on your device

The site saves a few small items in your browser so it works: a code for a time you’re holding while you book, and your sign-in if you have an account. We don’t use advertising cookies or tracking analytics.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and gift card records as long as needed for your visits, our tax and business records, and any disputes. Ask us and we’ll delete or remove what we don’t need to keep.

Your choices

  • See your visits and update your name and mobile in My account.
  • Ask us what we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by calling 504-522-2833 or visiting us. We may need to confirm it’s you first.

Security

We use encrypted connections, sign-in links instead of passwords, and access rules so clients see only their own visits and staff see only what their role allows. No system is perfectly secure; if your information is ever exposed, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

Children

This site is for adults. Anyone under 18 should be booked by a parent or guardian, who is responsible for the information they give us.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll post the new version here with a new date. Big changes will be noted on the booking page.
